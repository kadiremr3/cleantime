Questions

More answers on the Support page.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Clean Time works entirely on your iPhone. There is no account, no server and no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.

What if I delete a photo by mistake?

Open the Photos app › Albums › Recently Deleted. iOS keeps deleted photos there for 30 days.

Is Clean Time free?

Yes, up to 50 photos per cleanup. Clean Time Pro removes the limit and adds any speed, Clean by Date and Reverse mode. Pay once for Lifetime, or go weekly or monthly.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

In short: Clean Time collects no data. Your photos never leave your iPhone, there is no account, no tracking and no advertising.

Your photos

Clean Time needs access to your photo library to show your photos and, when you confirm, to delete the ones you chose. Photos are read on your device only. They are never uploaded, copied or shared.

Deleting always goes through the iOS confirmation dialog. Deleted photos move to the "Recently Deleted" album, where iOS keeps them for 30 days.

On-device analysis

Hints like "similar shot" and "looks blurry" are computed on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework. No image or result is sent anywhere.

What is stored on your device

  • Which photos you already reviewed and what you decided, so you don't see them twice.
  • Your settings (speed, filters and playback options).
  • Two counters shown in the app: how much storage you freed and how many cleanups you finished.
  • Whether Clean Time Pro is active, copied from the App Store so Pro features are ready at launch.

All of this stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Notifications

If you turn on Offload Protection, Clean Time schedules a weekly reminder on your device. It is a local notification: no server is involved and no data is sent.

Purchases

Clean Time Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; Clean Time only learns whether Pro is active. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID settings.

Data collection and third parties

Clean Time contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not collect, sell or share personal data. On the App Store this is shown as "Data Not Collected".

Children

Clean Time is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Need help? Write to support.cleantime@icloud.com. We usually answer within two working days.

How does Clean Time work?

Your photos play like a slideshow. Tap a photo to mark it for deletion; photos you don't tap are kept. Marked photos wait in Review until you confirm.

I deleted a photo by mistake

Open the Photos app › Albums › Recently Deleted. iOS keeps deleted photos there for 30 days, and you can recover them with one tap.

Clean Time doesn't show my photos

Open Settings › Apps › Clean Time › Photos and choose Full Access. With limited access, Clean Time only sees the photos you selected.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the About tab in Clean Time, tap Get Clean Time Pro, then tap Restore Purchases at the bottom. Use the same Apple ID you bought Pro with.

How do I switch plans, for example from monthly to lifetime?

Open the About tab in Clean Time and tap Change Plan. Your current plan is marked; pick another one and tap Switch Plan. After buying Lifetime, tap Cancel Old Subscription on the same tab so the subscription doesn't renew.

How do I cancel a weekly or monthly plan?

Open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Clean Time Pro › Cancel Subscription. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

What does Offload Protection do?

iOS can remove apps you haven't opened for a while. With Offload Protection on, Clean Time reminds you once a week to open it. Opening the app now and then makes it less likely that iOS offloads it.
